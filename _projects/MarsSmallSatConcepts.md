<i>Skills Used: MATLAB, thermal analysis, technology research, system engineering, power budgeting, mass budgeting, system design, technical writing</i>

<h3>My Contributions</h3>
<div>
    <ul>
        <li>Developed 2 Mars mission concepts to collect high feasibility and high precedence science for a multi-payload Mars mission study conducted by a working group in the Mars Exploration Program.</li>
        <li>Performed trade analysis in MATLAB to study heater power with instrument temperature requirements.</li>
        <li>Calculated limits for propulsive range to fulfill a SmallSat size constraint at Mars.</li>
        <li>Studied instrument suites that must withstand 70 m/s impact for hard lander concept.</li>
        <li>Presented technical detail and concepts of operations to international partners and gained insight to perform a high level trade study on a hypothetical delivery vehicle for the concepts.</li>
        <li>Presented my project to other interns at the end of summer seminar.</li>
    </ul>
</div>
